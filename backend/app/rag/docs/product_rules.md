# FinSight AI - Retail and Commercial Loan Product Rules (v2026.1)

## 1. Personal Loans (Unsecured)
- Loan Amounts: ₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000
- Tenure: 12 to 60 Months
- Minimum Net Monthly Income: ₹25,000 for Metro / Tier-1 cities, ₹20,000 for Tier-2+
- Interest Rate Range: 11.5% to 18.5% p.a. (risk-based pricing)
- Processing Fee: 1.5% to 2.5% + GST

## 2. MSME / Business Working Capital Loans
- Loan Amounts: ₹2,00,000 to ₹50,00,000
- Tenure: 12 to 84 Months
- Minimum Business Vintage: 2 years operational history with GST filing records
- Minimum Annual Turnover: ₹30,00,000
- Collateral: Hypothecation of business inventory and receivables for limits > ₹25 Lakhs
- Interest Rate Range: 10.0% to 16.0% p.a.

## 3. Vehicle & Auto Loans
- Loan Amounts: Up to ₹30,00,000
- Maximum Loan-To-Value (LTV): 85% for two-wheelers, 90% for commercial vehicles
- Tenure: 12 to 60 Months
- Comprehensive Vehicle Insurance and RTO Hypothecation Endorsement required prior to disbursement.
