# FinSight AI - Fair Collection Practices & Recovery Policy (v2026.1)

## 1. Regulatory Compliance (RBI Fair Practices Code)
- NBFC collections must strictly adhere to the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Fair Practices Code for Lenders.
- **Permissible Contact Hours**: Borrowers may only be contacted between 08:00 AM and 07:00 PM Indian Standard Time.
- **Harassment Prohibition**: Strict zero-tolerance policy against intimidation, abusive language, public shaming, or third-party workplace disclosures.
- **Authorized Recovery Channels**: Primary channels are Digital Voice Bot, SMS/WhatsApp Payment Links, and officially certified Field Collection Executives carrying biometric verification.

## 2. Priority Segmentation & Intervention Logic
- **Bucket 0 (Current / SMA-0, 1-30 DPD)**:
  - Focus on soft digital nudges (Automated WhatsApp reminders and UPI payment links 3 days before and 2 days after due date).
  - Do NOT deploy field recovery visits.
- **Bucket 1 (SMA-1, 31-60 DPD)**:
  - Telephonic relationship counseling and structured EMI repayment options.
  - Assess potential cash flow shocks (medical emergencies, seasonal business dip).
- **Bucket 2 (SMA-2, 61-90 DPD)**:
  - Senior collection manager outreach.
  - Evaluate loan restructuring, tenure re-amortization, or step-down EMI schedule if default probability is high but customer willingness to pay is demonstrated.
- **Bucket 3 (NPA, 90+ DPD)**:
  - Legal notice issuance under Section 138 (Cheque Bounce) / Section 25 (e-NACH bounce) of the Negotiable Instruments Act.
  - Ethical asset recovery according to SARFAESI guidelines for secured assets.

## 3. Vulnerability and Hardship Exceptions
- If an account exhibits extreme financial distress (illness, certified natural calamity), collections agent must freeze penalty interest charges and route to the hardship committee for moratorium evaluation.
