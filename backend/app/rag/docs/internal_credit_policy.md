# FinSight AI - Internal Credit Underwriting Policy (v2026.1)

## 1. Credit Score (CIBIL / Bureau) Thresholds
- **Tier 1 (Prime Approval)**: Bureau Score >= 720. Eligible for automated STP (Straight-Through-Processing) approvals up to ₹15,00,000 provided Debt-to-Income (DTI) is below 45%.
- **Tier 2 (Standard Review)**: Bureau Score 650 - 719. Requires standard verification. Maximum loan amount capped at 12x net monthly income.
- **Tier 3 (Enhanced Due Diligence / Review Required)**: Bureau Score 600 - 649. Requires manual underwriter sign-off, secondary guarantor or asset collateral, and bank statement verification.
- **Tier 4 (Policy Exclusion / Decline)**: Bureau Score < 600. Direct rejection unless secured by 100% liquid cash collateral (FD/Gold).

## 2. Debt-To-Income (DTI) & Financial Ratios
- **Maximum Permissible DTI**: 55% for salaried individuals, 60% for professional self-employed applicants.
- **DTI Calculation**: `Total Monthly EMIs (Existing + Proposed) / Net Monthly In-Hand Income`.
- Applications with DTI exceeding 55% must be routed to `REVIEW REQUIRED` with maximum tenure extension recommended to compress monthly outflow.

## 3. Delinquency and Past Performance
- **30+ DPD (Days Past Due)**: Maximum 1 instance permitted in preceding 12 months.
- **90+ DPD (NPA / Default)**: Zero tolerance in the preceding 24 months. Any write-off or settle tag mandates immediate `REJECT`.
- **Credit Utilization**: Revolving credit lines (credit cards/overdraft) utilization above 75% indicates stress and lowers approval probability.

## 4. Loan Sizing Limits
- Personal Loans: ₹50,000 to ₹10,00,000. Maximum tenure: 60 months.
- MSME Business Loans: ₹2,00,000 to ₹50,00,000. Maximum tenure: 84 months.
- Two-Wheeler / Auto Loans: ₹30,00,000 max. Minimum 15% borrower margin money (LTV <= 85%).
